A TET navigation app that stays on the phone

GPX Tracker is a free iPhone app for Trans Euro Trail navigation and any motorcycle GPX you import. Download official TET country files, keep offline maps on the device, and ride without a signal or an account.

Does it work for the Trans Euro Trail?
Yes. Download official Trans Euro Trail tracks from transeurotrail.org onto the phone and keep them up to date.
Can I use my own GPX?
Yes. Import a GPX file and navigate it on the iPhone. The file stays on the device.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Save OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap tiles before you ride. They stay on the phone when the signal drops.
Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts, no cloud for your rides, and no ads. See privacy or support if you need more.
